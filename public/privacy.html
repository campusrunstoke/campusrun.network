privacy policy

last updated: july 19, 2026

This policy explains what campus run ("we," "us") collects on campusrun.network, how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect what you give us to set up campaigns, we don't sell your personal information, and you can email us anytime to see or delete what we have.

what we collect

  • What you give us: your name, email, phone number, company or school details, and anything you write in our forms or send us by email or text.
  • Campaign details: the schools, dates, deliverables, and payments involved in your runs.
  • Basic usage data: device and browser type, pages visited, and similar technical data, including through cookies if analytics are enabled.

how we use it

  • To respond to you and set up, run, and manage campaigns.
  • To invoice brands, pay students, and keep records of runs.
  • To send you emails or texts about campaigns and campus dates, if you opted in.
  • To keep the site working, secure, and improving.

what we share

  • The other side of your campaign: brands see the students working their runs and their deliverables; students see the brand and run details.
  • Service providers: companies that help us operate, like hosting, email, payments, and analytics, only for the work they do for us.
  • Legal reasons: if the law requires it or to protect our rights and users.

We do not sell your personal information.

marketing messages

We only send marketing emails or texts if you opted in. You can unsubscribe from emails or reply STOP to texts at any time. We'll still send you messages needed to run an active campaign, like scheduling and confirmations.

cookies

We use cookies and similar tools to keep the site working and understand how it's used. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings; parts of the site may work less well without them.

how long we keep things

We keep your information while your account or conversation with us is active, and as long as we need it for the purposes above or to meet legal requirements. After that we delete it or anonymize it.

your choices

Email kasey@campusrun.network to access, correct, or delete your information. If you're a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and correct personal information; we honor those requests and won't treat you differently for making them.

kids

Campus run is for people 18 and older, and the site isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13, and we'll delete it if we learn we have.

security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't promise absolute security.

changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date.

contact

Privacy questions: kasey@campusrun.network
