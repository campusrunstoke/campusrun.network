Campus Run turns live events into measured, opted-in audiences. For the brands sampling at your event and the organizers running it, it grows an opted-in list on the spot and proves what the event actually drove.

The one sentence

Every giveaway, booth, or connect moment at a live event becomes a tracked touchpoint, so you grow an opted-in list on the spot and see what actually worked that night instead of hoping.

Why it works

Live events become list growth. People join the list from their own phone right when they're most engaged, in a single step. Far less friction than a paper card or a form, so far more people actually join.
You learn what worked, that night. A code unique to each event ties the physical moment to what happens next, so you stop guessing at what an event drove.
The list is yours, and everyone opted in. Every contact joined by their own hand. That's a clean, consent-first list you own, which beats anything scraped or rented on deliverability and on reputation.
One team, tech and boots. The same shop builds the software and staffs the event, so the data layer and the people handing out product are never two vendors pointing at each other.

How it works

  1. Capture. A quick touchpoint at any table, booth, or handout gets someone onto the list from their own phone in one step.
  2. Opt in. They join the list and answer a question or two, with a quick A/B test if you want it.
  3. Watch live. Signups, answers, and the best-performing spots stream to a dashboard during the event.
  4. Attribute. A per-event code shows what the moment drove in the days after, in real numbers.

Where it fits your events

  • Conferences and trade shows. Capture and measure the room in real time, and hand sponsors the numbers the same night.
  • Festivals and campus events. More opt-ins per hour than a folding table, with the follow-up list to match.
  • Sampling and product launches. Turn a giveaway into a lasting, opted-in relationship, and prove the reach.

Proof it's real

  • Piloting at a 3,000-person festival this summer, capturing the gate line with live surveys.
  • Partnered with a Mark Cuban backed startup for a fall campus launch, about 4,000 units across two weeks.
  • Real-time dashboard and consent-first capture already built and running.
